Under the hood

How a draw works

$GTA6 runs on top of KittyMine, a "proof-of-game" network whose blocks are mined by people playing games. We use its blocks as a fair, public clock and dice roll.

1

A block drops

About once a minute KittyMine makes a new block with a random hash that nobody can predict or pick.

2

Who's in?

We check KittyMine's Steam and Xbox friend lists for everyone in GTA at that moment.

3

One winner

The hash picks one player, who wins 1,000 $GTA6. No caps: fewer players means more wins each.

4

Cash in

The win lands in your stash straight away. Collect it to your wallet, then withdraw or export.

Want the exact formula, or to check a draw yourself? Read the Docs.

Stuck?

Common problems

"Am I in the draw?" says it can't see me

Nine times out of ten it's one of these: KittyMine hasn't accepted your friend request yet (requests are accepted by hand, so ask in the Discord), your game details or online status are private, or you're set to Invisible / Appear offline. Fix it, keep GTA running, and check again after a minute or two.

I added "KittyMine" on Xbox

That's a different account. Remove it and add TheKittyMine.

The login code won't go through

Make sure you saved your profile after pasting the code, with no extra spaces. Steam and Xbox can take up to a minute to show the change. Codes expire, so if it's been a while, get a new one.

Do I need SOL or Phantom?

No. Your wallet is made for you, and we pay the network fee when you collect or withdraw. You only need your own wallet if you want to move your $GTA6 out.

I play on both Steam and Xbox

Do both setups. Each platform account gets its own wallet and its own chances to win.

Does my old GTA V time carry over to GTA VI?

Your Xbox account and wallet carry over. You don't redo anything. Just start GTA VI instead of GTA V.
