Side job

Trade KMCoin for $GTA6

Mined KMCoin on KittyMine? Send it to our KittyMine wallet and get … $GTA6 per KMCoin, credited to your stash automatically.

How the trade works. KMCoin lives on KittyMine's own blockchain, not Solana, so only you can move it, with your KMCoin password. You send it to our KittyMine wallet 548645 like any KittyMine transfer. Our server reads KittyMine's public ledger every 30 seconds, sees "wallet X sent N KMCoin to 548645", finds the Steam/Xbox account that owns wallet X, and credits it N × the rate in $GTA6. Watch it land on KittyMine's transactions page.

  1. Open the KMCoin Player (download it from KittyMine), load your wallet and enter your password.
  2. Choose Transfer, receiver 548645 , and the amount.
  3. Publish it like any KittyMine transaction (Xbox: your Bio. Steam: Real Name proof + an Xbox Bio). That's KittyMine's rule: sending KMCoin always needs an Xbox Bio.
  4. Once KittyMine confirms it (a few minutes), the $GTA6 shows up under Winnings to collect in My stash.

One-way and final: the KMCoin stays in wallet 548645. Trades are paid from a separate trade pool, never from the mining rewards.

FAQ

Questions

Where do the $GTA6 rewards come from?

A reward pool of $GTA6 sits in a public wallet: …. It only holds $GTA6 and a little SOL for fees. Each payout comes out of it. Check it on Solscan any time.

Does GTA VI count?

Yes, on Xbox Series X|S, from launch day (November 19). GTA VI isn't on PC, and we can't see PlayStation, so Xbox is the way to mine with GTA VI. Until then, GTA V on Xbox or Steam earns the same way, and your account keeps working when you switch games.

Why do I have to friend KittyMine?

KittyMine is a proof-of-game network (thekittymine.com). Its Steam and Xbox friend lists are our player list: we look at which of those friends are in GTA right now. KittyMine's blocks (about one a minute) are the clock and the fair coin flip for each draw.

What is KittyMine, and do I get KMCoin too?

KittyMine is a separate project with its own coin, KMCoin, that you mine by playing games while friended to its account. This site only pays $GTA6, but since you're friended to KittyMine anyway, your GTA time can also win KittyMine blocks. Learn more at thekittymine.com. We're not run by KittyMine.

How much can I earn?

Every win pays 1,000 $GTA6, with no daily cap and no limit on how often you can win. There's a draw about every minute, so the fewer people playing, the more often you win. If you're the only one in-game, you win every draw. It keeps paying until the 100M reward pool is empty.

Is the draw fair?

Each winner is picked from the KittyMine block hash, which nobody can choose in advance. Every draw (block, winner, how many were in it) is shown live on this page, and you can compare the block numbers with KittyMine's explorer.

I won before logging in. Did I lose it?

No. Winnings are saved under your Steam/Xbox account. Log in any time and collect them.

Is "Sign in through Steam" safe?

Yes. It sends you to Steam's own website (steamcommunity.com) to log in. We never see your Steam password; Steam only tells us your Steam ID.

Who controls my $GTA6 wallet?

The site creates it for you and keeps the key encrypted, so you can play without any crypto setup. For full control, use Export private key and import it into Phantom, or withdraw to your own wallet. Don't leave large amounts in a site-held wallet.
