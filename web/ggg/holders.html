How it's split

Your share = your bag.

  1. 1

    Counted every minute

    Every minute we take the 100 biggest $GTA6 wallets. Pools, the reward pool and the team wallets don't count, so it's always 100 real holders.

  2. 2

    Share of the top 100

    Your share is your bag divided by everything the top 100 hold together. If the top 100 hold 70% of the supply and you hold 3%, you get 3 ÷ 70 = 4.29% of every payout.

  3. 3

    Paid every 5 minutes

    Every 5 minutes the fees are claimed and split over the five minute snapshots, so holding all the time beats buying right before a payout.

  4. 4

    Straight to your wallet

    TTWO lands in the wallet that holds your $GTA6. Shares under $0.10 roll over to the next round so they aren't eaten by fees.

your payout = round fees × (your $GTA6 ÷ $GTA6 held by the top 100)

Trust

What you're trusting.

The 50/30/20 split is locked on-chain by pump.fun. The holder payouts are sent by our server from the holder wallet, which anyone can watch on Solscan: every fee that comes in and every payout that goes out. Each round lists a fingerprint (hash) of the holder list it used. A little TTWO is swapped for SOL now and then to pay network fees, and that's shown in the totals. TTWO is issued by Backpack, which can freeze or pause its token, as with any tokenized stock. More in the Docs.
