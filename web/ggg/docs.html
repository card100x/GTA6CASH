Documentation

Get paid to play GTA.

Everything about how $GTA6 works: how you earn, how winners are picked, where the money comes from, and what can go wrong.

Overview

$GTA6 is a community memecoin on Solana that you earn by playing GTA. While you're in the game, you're entered in a draw that runs about once a minute. Each draw pays one player in $GTA6 from a public reward pool.

The pieces:

  • Your Steam or Xbox status shows that you're in GTA right now.
  • A KittyMine block, made about once a minute, is the clock and the dice roll. Nobody can predict or choose its hash.
  • The reward pool is a public Solana wallet holding the $GTA6 that gets paid out.
  • The relay is our server. It watches the blocks, checks who's in-game, picks the winner and records the win.

No mods, no downloads, nothing installed on your PC or console. You play like normal.

Quick start

The illustrated step-by-step guide for each platform is on Play to Earn. The short version:

  1. Friend KittyMine. Steam: add KittyMineCoin. Xbox: add the gamertag TheKittyMine (watch the spelling: "KittyMine" without "The" is a different account). Wait until it's accepted.
  2. Make your game status public. See Privacy settings.
  3. Log in on gtasix.cash with Steam or Xbox. A $GTA6 wallet is made for you.
  4. Play GTA. Wins show up under "Winnings to collect". Collect them, then withdraw to any Solana wallet or export the key.

You can start playing before you log in. Wins are saved to your Steam or Xbox account and wait there until you log in and collect.

Privacy settings

We can only see you if KittyMine's account is allowed to see what you're playing.

Steam

  1. Profile → Edit Profile → Privacy Settings.
  2. Set My profile and Game details to Public.
  3. Don't set yourself to "Invisible" in the friends list. Online, Away and Busy all work.

Xbox

  1. Settings → Account → Privacy & online safety → Xbox privacy → View details & customize.
  2. Under Online status & history, set "Others can see if you're online" and "Others can see what you're watching and playing" to Everybody.
  3. Don't "Appear offline".

Logging in

Logging in links your gaming account to a $GTA6 wallet. There's one wallet per Steam or Xbox account.

Steam, option 1: Sign in through Steam

You're sent to Steam's own site (steamcommunity.com) to log in. Steam only tells us your Steam ID. We never see your password.

Steam, option 2: a code in your Real Name

Enter your Steam ID (find it at steamid.io). You get a short code. Put it in your Steam profile's Real Name field, then press verify. You can remove it once you're in.

Xbox: a code in your Bio

Enter your gamertag. You get a short code. Put it in your Xbox Bio (Xbox app → Profile → Customize → Bio), then press verify. You can remove it once you're in.

Why a code? It proves you control that profile without ever giving us a password.

Your wallet

When you log in, the site creates a Solana wallet for you so you can earn without any crypto setup. Its private key is stored encrypted on our server.

Collect

Wins first appear as Winnings to collect, a balance recorded under your Steam or Xbox account. Collect sends them from the reward pool to your wallet as real $GTA6 on Solana.

Withdraw

Send $GTA6 from your wallet to any Solana address, such as your Phantom wallet or an exchange that supports $GTA6. You don't need SOL: the pool pays the network fee. Limit: 10 withdrawals a day.

Export private key

Shows your wallet's private key so you can import it into Phantom and control it yourself. Anyone who sees that key can take everything in the wallet. Never share it, and never paste it anywhere except your own wallet app.

Don't store a lot here. The site wallet is a convenience, not a vault. Withdraw or export regularly.

How draws work

Every time KittyMine makes a new block (about once a minute):

  1. Who's in? We read KittyMine's Steam and Xbox friend lists and take everyone whose status shows a supported game right now.
  2. Sort. That list of players is sorted into a fixed order, so the same players always give the same list.
  3. Roll. The block's hash, combined with the $GTA6 contract address, picks one position in the list:
winner = players[ sha256(blockHash + ":" + CA) mod playerCount ]

Because nobody knows the block hash before the block exists, nobody (including us) can steer who wins.

How much a draw pays

Every win pays a fixed 1,000 $GTA6. There's no daily budget, no cap per player, and no end date: draws keep paying 1,000 each until the 100M reward pool is empty.

KittyMine makes a block about once a minute, so at most about 1,300 draws happen a day. Fewer players means more wins for each of them. If you're the only one in-game, you win every draw. Live numbers are on the homepage.

KittyMine "Legacy" blocks (from its old system) are skipped. If nobody is in-game, that block pays nobody.

Check a draw yourself

Every draw is listed live on the homepage with its block number, the winner and how many players were in it.

  1. Look up the block number on KittyMine's explorer and copy its hash.
  2. Compute sha256(hash + ":" + CA), read it as one big number, and take the remainder after dividing by the player count. That's the winner's position in the sorted list.
  3. For each draw we also save a fingerprint (hash) of the player list we used, so the list can't be quietly changed later.

What you can't check from outside is who was really in-game at that moment. That comes from Steam and Xbox, and only our server sees it. See Trust & risks.

The reward pool

The reward pool is a regular Solana wallet that holds the $GTA6 to be paid out, plus a little SOL for network fees. Every collect and every withdrawal fee comes out of it, and anyone can watch it on Solscan.

Pool walletloading…Solscan

The pool pays out over a set period, so the daily amount is fixed in advance. "Winnings to collect" are a promise against this pool until you collect them. Once collected, the $GTA6 is in your own wallet.

Fair play

  • One wallet per Steam account and one per Xbox account.
  • Everyone in-game at a block has the same chance. Playtime before that block doesn't matter.
  • Idling in the menu still shows as in-game, and that counts. Being friended and offline doesn't.

The relay also has anti-farming checks we can switch on if someone tries to game it with lots of fake accounts:

  • Account age: new Steam accounts can't win.
  • Two checks in a row: you must be seen in-game in two status checks back to back, so popping in for a second doesn't count.
  • Daily win cap: a limit on wins per account per day.

Right now these are off. If we switch any on, we'll announce it on X first.

KittyMine

KittyMine is a separate "proof-of-game" network: its blocks are mined by people playing games while friended to its Steam or Xbox account, not by computers solving puzzles. It has its own coin, KMCoin.

We use KittyMine's public blocks as a fair clock and dice roll, and its friend lists as our player list. Since you're friended to KittyMine anyway, your GTA time can also win KittyMine blocks and KMCoin, on top of $GTA6.

Curious how a blockchain can run on gamers? Ask in the Discord.

$GTA6 isn't run by KittyMine, and KittyMine doesn't hold or pay $GTA6.

Trust & risks

Here's what you're trusting:

  • Our server holds keys. The reward pool and the site wallets are controlled by keys on our server. If the server were hacked or the team went bad, those funds could be lost. That's why the site says to withdraw or export instead of leaving large amounts.
  • Who was in-game is reported by us. The dice roll is public, but the player list comes from Steam and Xbox through our server. We save a fingerprint of every list, but you can't check it against Steam or Xbox yourself.
  • We depend on Steam, Xbox and KittyMine. If any of them goes down or changes how it works, draws can pause.
  • The pool is finite. When it runs out, payouts stop unless it's refilled.
  • It's a memecoin. The price can go to zero. Only use money you can afford to lose.

Next, we want to move more of this on-chain, so that claims are paid by a Solana program instead of a server key.

Troubleshooting

I'm playing but never show up as in-game

  • Check that KittyMine actually accepted your friend request: it should be in your friends list, not "pending". Requests are accepted by hand, so post your Steam name or gamertag in the KittyMine Discord.
  • Check your privacy settings. This is the most common cause.
  • Xbox: make sure you added TheKittyMine, not "KittyMine".
  • Steam: the game has to be launched through Steam so it shows as "Playing".
  • Status updates every minute or so. Give it a couple of minutes after you start the game.
  • Check whether KittyMine sees you at all: your ID should appear on its player info page once you're friends and playing.

The Steam or Xbox code won't verify

Save your profile after pasting the code, then wait a minute: Steam and Xbox can take a moment to show the change. Make sure the code is exact, with no extra spaces.

A withdrawal failed

Check the address is a Solana address, not an ETH one or another chain's. If it still fails, the pool may be low on SOL for fees. Tell us on X.
